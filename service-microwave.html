Service Details

This page covers microwaves, stoves and ovens, including gas stove repair needs. We help with heating issues, faulty elements, broken igniters, unsafe burners, and controls that stop your kitchen from working properly.

Microwave issues

  • Not heating food
  • Sparking or arcing
  • Turntable not rotating
  • Door latch faults
  • Display and button failures

Stove & oven issues

  • Broken elements and poor heating
  • Gas burner and ignition problems
  • Oven not reaching temperature
  • Control switch faults
  • Uneven cooking and thermostat issues
