Microwave, Stove & Oven Repairs in Cape Town

On-site microwave, stove and oven repair across Cape Town, Kuils River, Bellville and Brackenfell.

Service Details

We repair microwaves, stoves, ovens and gas stoves across Cape Town, Kuils River, Bellville, Brackenfell and Mitchells Plain. Common faults include microwaves that won't heat, sparking interiors, turntables that have stopped, broken door latches, faulty elements, igniter problems, and thermostat issues. We repair Defy, Samsung, Bosch, LG, Whirlpool, Hisense and Kelvinator appliances.

Call-out fee from R250 depending on distance. All repair work is backed by our workmanship guarantee. WhatsApp us your appliance model and fault for a quick quote.
