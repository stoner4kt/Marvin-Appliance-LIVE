Appliance Repair Service Areas in Cape Town

On-site repairs across Kuils River, Bellville, Brackenfell, Mitchells Plain, Somerset West, Stellenbosch and the wider Cape Town metro.

Marvin's Appliance Repairs is based in Kuils River (Kuilsrivier), Western Cape. We travel to homes across the Cape Town metro area for on-site appliance repair. Call-out fee starts from R250 depending on distance. We repair fridges, washing machines, microwaves, stoves, ovens, dishwashers, gas stoves, geysers, tumble dryers, and ice machines.

To book a repair, WhatsApp or call us on 060 325 6033 with your appliance type, suburb, and the fault description.
