Service Details

Don't let a broken refrigerator spoil your food. Our certified technicians diagnose and repair all cooling system issues fast. We handle compressor problems, thermostat failures, leaks, and electrical faults for all major brands including Samsung, LG, Defy, Whirlpool, and more.

Common Issues We Fix:

  • Not cooling or freezing properly
  • Water leaking inside or outside
  • Strange noises or loud compressor
  • Ice maker not working
  • Door seal/gasket problems
  • Temperature control issues
  • Frost buildup in freezer
