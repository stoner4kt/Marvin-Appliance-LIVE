❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our repair services

How much does appliance repair cost? +

Our call-out fee is R450, which includes diagnosis. Repair costs depend on the issue and parts needed. We provide a upfront quote before starting any work - no hidden fees. Most repairs range between R650 - R2500. We also offer a 'repair or replace' assessment if your appliance is old.

Do you offer same-day service? +

Yes! For bookings made before 12 PM, we offer same-day service depending on your location and our schedule. Emergency repairs are prioritized. You'll get a confirmation call within 30 minutes of booking online.

What brands do you repair? +

We repair all major brands including Samsung, LG, Defy, Whirlpool, Bosch, Siemens, Hisense, Kenwood, Russell Hobbs, and many more. If you don't see your brand listed, contact us - we likely still service it.

Is there a warranty on repairs? +

Absolutely. All our repairs come with a 90-day warranty on workmanship and replaced parts. If the same issue recurs within this period, we'll fix it free of charge. This warranty is valid for the specific repair performed and does not cover new, unrelated issues.

Do I need to be home during the repair? +

Yes, an adult (18+) needs to be present during the repair for access and payment authorization. The repair typically takes 1-2 hours depending on the complexity. We'll call 30 minutes before arrival so you don't have to wait around all day.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept cash, EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer), and all major credit/debit cards. Payment is due upon completion of the repair. For EFT, we require proof of payment before the technician leaves.

What areas do you service? +

We currently service Johannesburg, Pretoria, and surrounding areas within a 50km radius. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, WhatsApp us your location and we'll confirm immediately.

What if my appliance can't be repaired? +

If we determine your appliance is beyond economical repair (BER), we'll provide a written assessment for insurance purposes if needed. You only pay the diagnosis fee (R450). We'll also give you honest advice on whether replacement makes more sense than repair.

How do I prepare for the technician's visit? +

Please ensure the appliance is accessible (clear any items around it), have your appliance model number ready if possible, and ensure parking is available for our service vehicle. If it's a refrigerator, try to minimize opening it before we arrive to keep food cold.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking? +

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 4 hours before your appointment. Please call or WhatsApp us at 060 325 6033. Late cancellations (less than 4 hours notice) may incur a R200 fee.
