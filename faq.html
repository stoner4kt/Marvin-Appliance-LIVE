❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about pricing, repairs, guarantees, and supported services.

How much is the call-out fee?+

Our call-out fee starts from R250, depending on distance. We explain the applicable fee before confirming your booking.

What appliances do you repair?+

We repair washing machines, tumble dryers, fridge and freezers, stoves and ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, gas stoves, geysers, and ice machines.

Do you repair TVs?+

No. The website is now focused only on the appliance categories listed above, and TV repairs are intentionally excluded.

Is the repair work guaranteed?+

Yes. All completed repair work is backed by our workmanship guarantee, giving customers extra confidence when booking with us.

Can I book from my phone?+

Yes. The site layout and booking forms were designed to work cleanly on mobile, tablet, laptop, and larger screens.

Do you handle urgent repairs?+

Yes. Message or call us with the appliance type, your area, and the fault so we can guide you on the next available slot.
