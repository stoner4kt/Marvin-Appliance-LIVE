📄 Terms & Conditions

Last updated: March 23, 2026

1. Service Scope

Marvin Appliances provides repair services for washing machines, tumble dryers, fridge and freezers, stoves and ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, gas stoves, geysers, and ice machines.

TV repairs are not part of the service offering listed on this website.

2. Call-out Fees

The call-out fee starts from R250 and depends on distance. The applicable fee is communicated before the booking is confirmed.

3. Quotes and Approval

After diagnosis, we explain the recommended repair and associated cost before carrying out additional work. Repair work proceeds only once the customer agrees.

4. Workmanship Guarantee

All completed work is covered by our workmanship guarantee. This applies to the specific repair carried out and does not automatically extend to unrelated or newly developed faults.

5. Booking and Access

Customers should provide clear access to the appliance and accurate details about the fault and location. An adult should be present when access to the property or appliance is required.

6. Cancellation and Rescheduling

If you need to reschedule, please notify us as early as possible so we can assist efficiently and reduce unnecessary travel.

7. Privacy

We collect contact details, service address information, and appliance details only for booking, communication, and service delivery purposes. We do not sell your personal information.
