📄 Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2024

1. Service Agreement

By booking a repair service with Marvin Appliances, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. These terms govern the provision of appliance repair services, including diagnosis, repair work, parts replacement, and warranty coverage.

1.1 Service Description

Marvin Appliances provides professional repair services for household electronic appliances including refrigerators, washing machines, microwaves, ovens, televisions, and other related equipment. Services include diagnosis, repair, maintenance, and parts replacement as agreed upon.

1.2 Booking and Scheduling

Service appointments can be booked through our website, WhatsApp, or phone. While we strive to accommodate your preferred time slots, all appointments are subject to availability and confirmation. Same-day service is offered based on technician availability and is not guaranteed.

2. Pricing and Payment

2.1 Call-Out and Diagnosis Fee

A non-refundable call-out fee of R450 is charged for the technician to visit your location and diagnose the appliance. This fee is payable regardless of whether you proceed with the repair.

2.2 Repair Quotes

After diagnosis, you will receive a detailed quote for the repair including labor and parts costs. No repair work will commence without your explicit approval of the quote.

2.3 Payment Terms

Payment is due immediately upon completion of the repair. We accept cash, EFT, and credit/debit cards. For EFT payments, proof of payment must be provided before the technician departs. Failure to pay will result in the removal of installed parts and reporting to credit bureaus.

3. Warranty and Guarantees

3.1 Repair Warranty

All repairs carry a 90-day warranty on workmanship and replaced parts. If the same issue recurs within this period due to faulty workmanship or defective parts, we will repair it free of charge.

3.2 Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

  • New, unrelated issues with the appliance
  • Damage caused by misuse, negligence, or accidents after repair
  • Appliances used for commercial purposes (unless specified)
  • Cosmetic damage not affecting functionality
  • Consumable parts (filters, belts, etc.) after 30 days

3.3 Beyond Economical Repair (BER)

If an appliance is deemed beyond economical repair, you are liable for the diagnosis fee only. We will provide a written assessment if required for insurance purposes.

4. Customer Responsibilities

4.1 Access and Safety

You must ensure safe access to the appliance and provide accurate information about the fault. An adult (18+) must be present during the repair. We reserve the right to refuse service if the working environment is unsafe.

4.2 Data and Settings

For electronic appliances (TVs, smart devices), you are responsible for backing up any data or settings. We are not liable for loss of saved settings, channels, or personal data during repair.

5. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations or rescheduling must be made at least 4 hours before the appointment. Late cancellations may incur a R200 fee. If our technician arrives and cannot access the property or the appliance, the full call-out fee will be charged.

6. Limitation of Liability

Marvin Appliances' liability is limited to the cost of the repair service provided. We are not liable for:

  • Consequential damages (spoiled food, water damage, etc.)
  • Pre-existing damage to the appliance
  • Damage caused by manufacturer defects
  • Loss of use during repair period

7. Privacy Policy

7.1 Information Collection

We collect personal information including your name, contact details, address, and appliance details solely for the purpose of providing repair services. This information is collected through our booking forms, WhatsApp, or phone calls.

7.2 Information Use

Your information is used to:

  • Schedule and perform repair services

7.3 Information Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is stored securely and only accessible to authorized personnel.

7.4 Third-Party Sharing

We may share your information with:

7.5 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt-out of marketing communications

7.6 Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies to improve user experience and analyze traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.
